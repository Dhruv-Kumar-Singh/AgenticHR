# AI Interview Coach

**Practice technical interviews out loud, with an AI interviewer that actually explains your feedback.**

AI Interview Coach is a voice-based mock interview platform for technical interview prep. You speak
your answers to an AI interviewer just like you would in a real interview, and afterward you get a
clear, evidence-based report showing exactly what you did well and what to improve — not just a score.

---

## What it does

- **Talk through real interview questions** — no typing. An AI voice agent asks you questions and
  listens to your spoken answers, just like a live interviewer would.
- **Choose your topic** — practice DSA, DBMS, or Full-stack development questions, at the level
  you're targeting.
- **Get feedback you can actually trust** — every score in your report is backed by what you
  actually said in the interview.
- **Track your progress** — a dashboard shows your interview history and performance trends over
  time.
- **Export and share** — download your reports as PDF or CSV to keep for your records or share
  with a mentor or recruiter.

## How it works

1. **Sign up and log in** to your account.
2. **Start a mock interview** and pick a topic — DSA, DBMS, or Full-stack.
3. **Speak your answers** out loud as the AI interviewer asks you questions, one at a time,
   adapting the flow the way a real interviewer would.
4. **The AI evaluates your responses** against what a strong answer actually looks like, checking
   for correctness, clarity, and depth — not just keyword matching.
5. **Get your report** right after the interview — a clear breakdown of your performance, with
   every point of feedback tied directly to something specific you said.
6. **Review and improve** on your dashboard, where you can see your scores and trends across all
   your past sessions.

## Advantages over other platforms

- **You speak, you don't type.** Most prep tools are text-based question banks. This platform
  mirrors what an actual interview feels like — thinking on your feet and explaining yourself out
  loud — which is the skill that actually gets tested in real interviews.
- **Feedback that shows its work.** Instead of a plain "6/10" or "needs improvement," every score
  points back to the exact part of your answer that earned it. You always know *why*.
- **No vague or made-up feedback.** The system never tells you something you didn't actually
  demonstrate — good or bad. If it's not backed by your answer, it doesn't go in your report.
- **A human is always in the loop.** Recruiters and reviewers can look at any AI-generated score
  and override it with their own judgment and reasoning — the AI supports the decision, it doesn't
  make it for you.
- **One place to track growth over time.** Instead of one-off practice sessions with no memory,
  your dashboard shows how you're trending across topics and attempts.
- **Covers the topics that matter most.** Purpose-built around the core areas technical interviews
  actually test — DSA, DBMS, and Full-stack — rather than generic, unfocused question sets.

## Who should use it

- **Candidates and students** preparing for technical interviews who want realistic, repeatable
  voice practice without needing to schedule a human interviewer.
- **Job seekers switching roles or leveling up**, who want targeted practice in DSA, DBMS, or
  Full-stack topics before a real interview.
- **Recruiters and hiring managers** who want a consistent, explainable way to assess a candidate's
  technical performance before committing further interview rounds.
- **Career coaches, bootcamps, and mentors** who want an objective, evidence-based tool to
  supplement the feedback they give their students or mentees.
